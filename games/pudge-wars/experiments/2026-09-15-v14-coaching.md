## Protocol and limitations

The [plain-text IR](../../../coaching/policies/v14/policy.md) uses stable block
IDs, provenance and inline evidence. The original oracle is immutable.
Each hypothesis was written before compilation. Unknown semantic edits fail
the bounded compiler; compiled outputs remain candidates until verified.
Candidate provenance includes IR, compiler, library and policy hashes.
The compiler now archives its input sources as well. Earlier candidate
code and compiler hashes are retained; subsequent evidence annotations do
not alter the registered semantic digest.

Probes cover named decision projections with at least 99% lift agreement
and zero permitted isolation leaks. During full games a shadow oracle
receives each candidate observation; changed actions must stay in the
specified region, and existing memory must match. These checks establish
local behavioral differences. They do not empirically lift the rest of
the policy's semantics or infer its goals. The broader residual is
unmeasured, not zero. Repeated compilations use the same deterministic
compiler, so their agreement is not evidence about independent natural-
language interpretations.

Each screen uses twelve fresh seeds on both banks, 24 complete games,
three identical policies per team, a 30-kill goal and 38,400-tick limit.
Intervals resample twelve seed blocks, averaging both banks within each,
with 10,000 bootstrap draws and RNG seed 9152026. The native JSON adapter
is synchronous; hosted reply timing is not modeled. These are head-to-head
screens against the frozen oracle, not separate fixed-opponent baseline
and candidate arms. No local result establishes a hosted leaderboard gain.
Candidate selection still requires fresh confirmation and hosted transfer.

Blink's first compilation failed 32 boundary/isolation probes and was
discarded before evaluation. The corrected compilation passed 3,456 probes
and 1,221,687 matched-observation decisions. The predicate, motion model and
consuming skill are one entangled dependency group; the reported effect is
their joint head-to-head ablation, not three separate causal estimates.
The experiment shows that geometric clearance alone does not justify
removing the broad defensive relocation.

Uncoordinated rune priority passed 350 probes and 1,188,420 matched-observation
decisions. It collected 416 runes versus the oracle's 369 in the same games:
a mean difference of +1.96 per game, interval [+0.13, +3.71]. This is a
descriptive head-to-head difference, not a controlled change in collection
against a third opponent. The kill-margin interval still spans zero.

The narrower branch `v14-rune-003` assigned early rune priority to one locally
elected collector, preserving enemy-shot priority for other teammates. It
passed 350 priority probes, 24 ownership probes and 1,229,550 matched-observation
decisions. Its outcome interval still spans zero, and the observed margin
falls below the prior +2 target. The ownership predicate and consuming rule
are a joint dependency group; no independent effects are claimed for them.

All three interventions were reverted after **72 complete seeded games**.
No further intervention was selected from this diagnostic set. The loop stops
with v14 unchanged, ready to incorporate human coaching or new matchup
evidence. None of the reserved confirmation ranges was used, and no hosted
XP requests or policy uploads were made. The results do not establish that
these ideas can never work; they do not clear the acceptance bar here.
